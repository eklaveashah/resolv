What it does

Three things, every minute.

01

Reads everything, everywhere

Open contracts from both exchanges, and the news wire behind them, pulled into one place and refreshed every minute. Anything that isn't a live quote is labelled, with its age.

02

Works out what it means

Each headline is scored for tone and linked to the contracts it may bear on, so you see the context, not just a number. On live contracts a headline carries no weight until contract-specific evidence is validated. If we can't point to a reason, we don't make a call.

03

Shows the gap, and the limit

Where a validated model and the market disagree, you see by how much and why, alongside the most a disciplined position would ever risk. Until a model qualifies, live contracts show the market price and stay on hold. What you do is your call.

The obvious question

Aren't these markets
already efficient?

Mostly, yes. That is why our answer on most contracts is sit this one out, and why we would rather say that than manufacture a reason to trade. Where gaps do persist, it is for reasons that are boring and structural rather than clever.

01

Attention is the scarce thing

Hundreds of contracts are open at once and most have nobody watching them closely. We are not faster than a trading desk on the headline everyone is already reading. We cover the rest of the board, the part nobody has time for.

02

Two exchanges, one question

The same event can trade at different prices on Kalshi and Polymarket. Lined up side by side the gap is plain arithmetic, but only once both contracts are known to settle on the same rules, so live pairs need a reviewed match, and fees come off first.

03

Longshots run rich

Cheap, unlikely contracts are persistently overbought. It is one of the best documented patterns in betting markets and it has survived decades of people knowing about it, so it is the first correction our model tests. On held-out data so far, it has not beaten the market price.

So we are not claiming to outguess the crowd. We read more of the wire than you have time to, we show what it implies for each contract, and we say plainly when the market has it about right. See how we check ourselves →

Proof

We keep score.

Anyone can claim an edge. The honest test is whether a model beats the exchange's own price on contracts it has never seen. None of ours has yet, so we show the market price and say so.This server reports a model that has, on held-out data; its status is below, and live calls stay on hold until its live record can be judged.

What happens when the 14 days end?
Nothing, unless you choose a plan. There is no card on file, so the trial cannot turn into a charge. Your watchlist and logged positions stay exportable.
Can I switch between Basic and Pro?
Any time, in either direction. Moving up takes effect immediately and you pay the difference for the days left. Moving down takes effect at the next renewal.
What does annual billing change?
Only the price: you pay for ten months and get twelve. If you cancel an annual plan in the first 30 days you get all of it back.
Why is there no free plan?
Live exchange data and the news wire cost money to run, and a free tier would have to be paid for by something else, such as your attention or your data. We would rather charge a clear price and owe you nothing but the product.
