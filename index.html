What it does

Three things, every minute.

01

Finds the mispriced ones

We watch every open contract on both exchanges and flag the ones trading away from what the evidence says they're worth.

02

Shows you why

Every call comes with the headlines and numbers behind it. If we can't point to a reason, we don't make the call.

03

Sizes the bet

A suggested stake based on how big the edge is and how sure we are, capped so one bad call can't wipe you out.
